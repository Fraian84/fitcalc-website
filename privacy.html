Privacy Policy (English)

Last Updated: April 7, 2026

Your privacy is very important to us. This policy explains how FitTool collects, uses, and protects your personal data, fully compliant with Google Play Store policies.

1. Data Collection and Sharing

FitTool collects information you provide (Name, Weight, Height, Age) to calculate fitness metrics (BMI, TDEE). Sharing: Users can generate PDF reports and send them via their chosen email client; data is transmitted externally only in this case.

2. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: All fitness data is stored locally on your device. No personal data is uploaded to our servers.
  • Data Deletion: Users can remove records in-app or uninstall the app to delete their data.

3. Advertising and Device Identifiers

FitTool uses Google AdMob. AdMob collects the Android Advertising ID and diagnostic data for ad personalization and performance analytics, according to Google Policies.

4. Minors (13+)

The app is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. For minors aged 13–17, all data remains under local user control.

5. Contact

Questions? Contact us at the email listed on the Google Play Store page.
